AIR INDIA — REVIEWED ANSWERS

TRAVEL BOOKING STEPS
Step 1: Open the official Air India booking channel
Open a web browser or the Air India app and go to https://www.airindia.com/in/en/book.html.
You can also book through Air India's website or app, an Air India office/counter, or a travel agent. Use the provider's official channel so the ticket and support route match the selected provider.

Step 2: Enter the journey details
Enter your departure and destination airports/cities.
Choose the travel date and passenger count, then search for available flights.

Step 3: Compare available options
Compare airline, flight number, departure and arrival times, stops, airports/terminals, cabin, fare family, baggage, and seat availability.
Open the selected flight details to verify its route, timing, and fare conditions.

Step 4: Select a service and review its rules
Choose the flight and fare that fit your journey.
Before continuing, review Air India fare family and route-specific baggage/add-on conditions. Check what baggage is included and whether changes or cancellation are allowed.

Step 5: Enter passenger details
Enter each passenger's name as it appears on their travel ID and provide the requested contact details.
For international flights, verify passport, visa, transit, and destination entry requirements for your route and nationality before payment.
Review names, dates, and contact details carefully before continuing.

Step 6: Review the total and pay
Check the selected flight segments, passenger details, fare, taxes/fees, baggage, seats, and optional add-ons.
Pay using an option displayed by Air India. Wait for the booking result; do not refresh or submit payment again while it is processing.

Step 7: Confirm and save the booking
Confirm that payment resulted in a booking and save Air India ticket/itinerary and booking reference.
If confirmation is unclear, check the provider's booking history before trying to pay again.

Step 8: Check travel-day instructions
Before departure, check Air India's current Manage Booking, check-in, and route-specific flight instructions.
SOURCES: https://www.airindia.com/content/air-india/in/en/frequently-asked-questions/booking.html ; https://www.airindia.com/in/en/manage/booking.html

CANCELLATION PROCESS
1. If booked directly, open Manage Booking on Air India's website/app and retrieve the trip using the booking reference and passenger details.
2. Select the available cancellation/refund option and review the fare-specific deduction before submitting.
3. Save the cancellation reference and track the request using Air India's refund support.
4. If an agent or third-party portal issued the ticket, contact that seller to process the cancellation/refund.
5. For an Air India-cancelled flight, use Manage Booking or the re-accommodation option described in Air India's notice.
Refund eligibility, amount, and timing depend on fare, route, payment channel, and current conditions.
SOURCES: https://www.airindia.com/in/en/manage/booking.html ; https://www.airindia.com/in/en/manage/request-refund.html ; https://www.airindia.com/content/air-india/in/en/frequently-asked-questions/payment-refund-cancellations.html

TRAVEL DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
1. Carry the ticket/booking details and original photo ID required for the itinerary.
2. For international travel, verify passport validity, visa, transit, and entry requirements for your nationality and route with official authorities.
3. Review Air India's current check-in and travel-document instructions for each flight segment.
4. Check that passenger details match travel documents and keep required originals accessible.
Requirements vary by route and nationality; confirm current rules before departure.
SOURCES: https://www.airindia.com/content/air-india/in/en/frequently-asked-questions/booking.html ; https://www.airindia.com/in/en/travel-information.html

COMMON AIR INDIA POLICIES
1. Air India fare families have different flexibility; the fare conditions attached to your ticket determine cancellation and change charges.
2. For direct bookings, use Manage Booking to access available changes, cancellation, or refund requests.
3. For a ticket issued by a travel agent or another portal, contact that seller for cancellation and refund handling.
4. Baggage allowance and paid add-on rules depend on route and ticket; check the allowance displayed for your flight and current Air India baggage page.
5. Check Air India's official flight updates and the current travel instructions before departure.
