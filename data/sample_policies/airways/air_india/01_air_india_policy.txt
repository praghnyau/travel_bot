AIR INDIA POLICY GUIDE — REVIEWED 9 OCTOBER 2026

BOOKING CHANNELS
Air India lists its website/app, offices/counters, and travel agents as booking options. Fare family and route determine ticket conditions. Review the fare rules and retain the ticket and booking reference. Source: https://www.airindia.com/content/air-india/in/en/frequently-asked-questions/booking.html

MANAGE BOOKING AND REFUNDS
For eligible direct bookings, Air India directs customers to Manage Booking to request changes or refunds. Refund and change amounts depend on fare type, route, class, and timing. For tickets issued by a travel agent or third-party portal, contact that provider; some tickets issued by an Air India office must be handled by that office. Sources: https://www.airindia.com/in/en/manage/booking.html ; https://www.airindia.com/in/en/manage/request-refund.html ; https://www.airindia.com/content/air-india/in/en/frequently-asked-questions/payment-refund-cancellations.html

AIRLINE-INITIATED DISRUPTIONS
If Air India cancels or changes a flight, review the notification and the airline's self-service re-accommodation/refund options for that booking. Available options depend on the disruption and ticket. Source: https://www.airindia.com/content/air-india/in/en/frequently-asked-questions/payment-refund-cancellations.html

BAGGAGE
Air India's baggage allowance and excess baggage rules can depend on route, cabin, and ticket. Check the baggage allowance printed on the ticket and the current official baggage guidelines. Source: https://www.airindia.com/in/en/travel-information/baggage-guidelines.html

TRAVEL DOCUMENTS
Carry the identification required for your itinerary. International passport, visa, transit, and entry requirements depend on nationality and route; verify with official destination/transit authorities and review Air India's travel instructions. Source: https://www.airindia.com/in/en/travel-information.html
