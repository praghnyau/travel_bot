AIR TRAVEL — BOOKING, CHECK-IN AND BOARDING

BOOKING STEPS
1. Use the airline's official site/app or a recognized platform.
2. Enter origin, destination, dates, trip type, passenger count and cabin.
3. Compare flight times, stops, airports/terminals, fare family, baggage, seat/meal add-ons and cancellation/change terms.
4. Enter passenger names as required by the airline and travel documents; verify passport details for international trips.
5. Add contact and required passenger details.
6. Review base fare, taxes, platform fees, add-ons and total.
7. Pay and wait for confirmed ticket/itinerary/booking reference (often PNR).
8. Verify all flight segments and save itinerary, baggage terms and support details.

BEFORE TRAVEL
Check flight status, terminal and schedule changes. Carry accepted photo ID for domestic flights. International travellers must verify passport validity, visa/e-visa/ETA, transit visa and destination entry conditions with official sources. Check baggage rules for every segment. Request assistance in advance.

CHECK-IN
1. Use online/app check-in when offered, or the airline counter.
2. Retrieve the booking and enter requested details.
3. Select a seat if available; fees may apply.
4. Confirm travel-document and baggage information.
5. Obtain the boarding pass.
6. Drop checked baggage before the airline's cutoff.
7. Complete security and immigration where applicable.
8. Reach the gate early and monitor official screens.
Check-in, bag-drop and gate-closing deadlines vary by airline, airport, route and flight. Never invent a universal deadline.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION
A booking confirmation/itinerary is not necessarily a boarding pass. Check-in and boarding are separate steps.

CONNECTING FLIGHTS
Check if all legs are on one ticket, whether baggage is checked through, and whether immigration/security re-screening is required. Separate-ticket connections may not be protected if the first flight is delayed. Transit visa requirements depend on nationality and itinerary.
