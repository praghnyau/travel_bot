AIR TRAVEL — DOCUMENTS, BAGGAGE AND COMMON POLICIES

DOMESTIC INDIA
Carry valid accepted photo ID and ticket/boarding information according to airline/airport rules. Passenger name should match travel ID as required. Check check-in, bag-drop and gate deadlines.

INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL
Depending on nationality, destination, transit and itinerary, documents may include:
- Passport meeting required validity/page conditions
- Visa, e-visa or electronic travel authorization (if required)
- Transit visa/authorization (if required)
- Onward/return ticket, accommodation or proof of funds where required
- Health, vaccination, study/work, minor-travel or other supporting documents where required
Check official destination/transit government or embassy sources and the airline. A ticket does not guarantee entry; border authorities decide admission.

CABIN VS CHECKED BAGGAGE
Cabin baggage is carried into the aircraft cabin within the airline's limits. Checked baggage is handed to the airline. Allowance varies by airline, fare, cabin, route, status and ticket terms. Basic fares may include less baggage. Codeshare/connecting segments can differ. Excess/oversized baggage may cost extra or be refused.

RESTRICTED ITEMS
Special rules apply to dangerous goods, power banks/spare lithium batteries, aerosols, liquids, sharp items, medicines, sporting equipment and special devices. Check the current airline and airport-security lists. Never advise hiding a restricted item.

SPECIAL PASSENGERS
- Pets/service animals: ask the operating airline whether they are accepted and whether advance approval, a specific carrier, or travel documents are required.
- For infants, unaccompanied minors, wheelchair assistance, medical equipment, oxygen, medicines, pregnancy, musical instruments and sports equipment, check the airline's rules and arrange approval or assistance in advance.

BOARDING AND NO-SHOW
The gate can close before departure. Follow boarding-pass times and airline updates. Missing check-in or gate cutoff may lead to denied boarding/no-show treatment; change/refund depends on fare and applicable law.

FAQ
Q: Is baggage included?
A: Check fare family and itinerary; do not infer from airline name alone.
Q: Can I change a passenger name?
A: Typo correction and ticket transfer are different; check airline/issuing platform.
Q: Do I need a transit visa?
A: It depends on nationality, airport, route, terminal/baggage arrangements and rules. Verify official government sources.
Q: Missing/damaged baggage?
A: Report to airline baggage staff promptly, keep baggage tags/receipts/reference, and follow claim deadlines.
