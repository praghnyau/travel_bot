INDIGO POLICY GUIDE — REVIEWED 9 OCTOBER 2026

BOOKING CHANNELS
IndiGo lists its website, mobile app, call centre, airport counters, and participating travel agencies as booking channels. Fare-type terms can change baggage, seat, change, or cancellation conditions. Read the selected fare rules and keep the booking reference. Source: https://www.goindigo.in/information/how-to-book.html

MANAGE BOOKING
IndiGo's Manage Booking service supports eligible journey changes, cancellation, and add-ons. Retrieve the booking on the official site/app, review the terms and any amount due, and save the updated itinerary or cancellation record. Source: https://www.goindigo.in/account/my-bookings.html

CANCELLATION AND REFUND
IndiGo's Conditions of Carriage tie cancellation/change fees and refund eligibility to fare type, timing, route, and ticket conditions. Current fees are listed on the airline's Fees and Charges page. For agent-issued tickets, contact the issuing agent about refund handling. If IndiGo cancels or reschedules a flight, review Plan B for the options available to that booking. Sources: https://www.goindigo.in/information/conditions-of-carriage.html ; https://www.goindigo.in/information/fees-and-charges.html ; https://www.goindigo.in/travel-information/en/refunds.html

CHECK-IN AND BAGGAGE
Check current web check-in availability, deadlines, baggage allowance, and restricted-item rules on IndiGo's booking-information pages for the actual flight. Fare type and route can affect conditions. Sources: https://www.goindigo.in/content/indigo/in/en/booking-information.html ; https://www.goindigo.in/information/conditions-of-carriage.html

TRAVEL DOCUMENTS
Carry the original identification required for the itinerary. International passport, visa, transit, and entry requirements depend on nationality and route; confirm them with official authorities and review IndiGo's current travel mandates. Sources: https://www.goindigo.in/information/conditions-of-carriage.html ; https://www.goindigo.in/content/indigo/in/en/booking-information.html
