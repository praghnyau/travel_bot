INDIGO — REVIEWED ANSWERS

TRAVEL BOOKING STEPS
Step 1: Open the official IndiGo booking channel
Open a web browser or the IndiGo app and go to https://www.goindigo.in/.
You can also book through IndiGo's website or app, call centre, airport counter, or a participating travel agency. Use the provider's official channel so the ticket and support route match the selected provider.

Step 2: Enter the journey details
Enter your departure and destination airports/cities.
Choose the travel date and passenger count, then search for available flights.

Step 3: Compare available options
Compare airline, flight number, departure and arrival times, stops, airports/terminals, cabin, fare family, baggage, and seat availability.
Open the selected flight details to verify its route, timing, and fare conditions.

Step 4: Select a service and review its rules
Choose the flight and fare that fit your journey.
Before continuing, review IndiGo fare type, baggage, seat, change, and cancellation rules for the selected flight. Check what baggage is included and whether changes or cancellation are allowed.

Step 5: Enter passenger details
Enter each passenger's name as it appears on their travel ID and provide the requested contact details.
For international flights, verify passport, visa, transit, and destination entry requirements for your route and nationality before payment.
Review names, dates, and contact details carefully before continuing.

Step 6: Review the total and pay
Check the selected flight segments, passenger details, fare, taxes/fees, baggage, seats, and optional add-ons.
Pay using an option displayed by IndiGo. Wait for the booking result; do not refresh or submit payment again while it is processing.

Step 7: Confirm and save the booking
Confirm that payment resulted in a booking and save IndiGo itinerary and booking reference.
If confirmation is unclear, check the provider's booking history before trying to pay again.

Step 8: Check travel-day instructions
Before departure, check IndiGo's current Manage Booking, web check-in, and route-specific flight instructions.
SOURCES: https://www.goindigo.in/information/how-to-book.html ; https://www.goindigo.in/account/my-bookings.html

CANCELLATION PROCESS
1. Open Manage Booking on IndiGo's website/app and retrieve the booking using the requested reference and passenger details.
2. Choose cancellation or change and review the fare-type rules, applicable fee, and any fare difference before confirming.
3. Save the cancellation confirmation and check the refund status using IndiGo's refund information.
4. If a travel agent issued the ticket, contact that agent about the cancellation and refund.
5. For an IndiGo-cancelled or rescheduled flight, check Plan B for the choices available for that booking.
Fees, deadlines, and refund options depend on fare, route, booking channel, and current terms.
SOURCES: https://www.goindigo.in/account/my-bookings.html ; https://www.goindigo.in/information/conditions-of-carriage.html ; https://www.goindigo.in/travel-information/en/refunds.html

TRAVEL DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
1. Carry the valid original photo ID required for the journey and keep your ticket/booking details available.
2. For international travel, verify passport, visa, transit, and destination entry requirements for your nationality and itinerary with official authorities.
3. Check IndiGo's current travel mandates and itinerary instructions for route-specific document checks.
4. Confirm passenger names and details against the travel documents before departure; ask IndiGo how to handle a correction.
Requirements vary by route and nationality; confirm current rules before departure.
SOURCES: https://www.goindigo.in/information/conditions-of-carriage.html ; https://www.goindigo.in/content/indigo/in/en/booking-information.html

COMMON INDIGO POLICIES
1. IndiGo fare types can have different baggage, seat, change, and cancellation conditions; the fare rules attached to the ticket control.
2. Use Manage Booking for supported changes, cancellations, and add-ons; check the current fee table before confirming a paid change.
3. Refund route depends on the original booking channel; for agent-issued tickets, contact the agent.
4. If IndiGo cancels or reschedules a flight, review the available Plan B options for the booking.
5. Check the current baggage page and restricted-item guidance for your flight rather than relying on a generic allowance.
