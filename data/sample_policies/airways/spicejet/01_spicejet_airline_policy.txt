SPICEJET POLICY GUIDE — REVIEWED 9 OCTOBER 2026

BOOKING CHANNELS
SpiceJet lists its official website/app, reservation centre, airport ticketing offices, and participating travel agents as booking channels. Review fare rules and any add-ons before payment. Keep the booking reference and itinerary. Source: https://corporate.spicejet.com/flighttticketbbookingfaq.aspx

CHANGES, CANCELLATIONS AND REFUNDS
Use Manage Booking on the SpiceJet website/app for eligible self-service changes and cancellations. SpiceJet's terms state that the fee depends on the applicable fare conditions and may be accompanied by a fare difference. Current fees vary and can change, so check the current fee table for the exact booking before confirming. When a ticket was purchased through an agent or portal, contact that seller for refund handling. SpiceJet says refund status can be checked through Manage Booking/Transaction Summary. Sources: https://corporate.spicejet.com/Tnc.aspx ; https://corporate.spicejet.com/PassengerRights.aspx ; https://corporate.spicejet.com/FeesCharges.aspx

CHECK-IN AND DOCUMENT CHECKS
SpiceJet offers Web Check-in through its website/app. Its FAQ says international passengers departing India must also report to the airport counter for document verification. Check the airline's current cutoff and flight instructions; do not rely on an old cutoff copied from a general guide. Source: https://corporate.spicejet.com/WebCheckinFaq.aspx

BAGGAGE
SpiceJet's baggage page distinguishes cabin and checked baggage and has route-specific allowance/charge tables. The allowance shown for the passenger's itinerary and the live baggage page control. Check restricted-item rules before packing. Sources: https://corporate.spicejet.com/Baggage.aspx ; https://corporate.spicejet.com/DGR.aspx

TRAVEL DOCUMENTS
SpiceJet requires passengers to present appropriate identification. For international travel, confirm passport validity, visa, transit, and destination entry rules with the official destination authorities; requirements depend on nationality and itinerary. Source: https://corporate.spicejet.com/Tnc.aspx
