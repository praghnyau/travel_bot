AIR TRAVEL — CANCELLATION, REFUNDS, CHANGES AND DISRUPTIONS

PASSENGER CANCELLATION
1. Open the airline or booking platform used.
2. Go to Manage Booking/My Trips and retrieve the booking.
3. Check every passenger and flight segment.
4. Review cancellation eligibility, penalty, refundable taxes/charges, non-refundable fees and refund method.
5. If booked through an agent/platform, the cancellation may need to be handled by that channel.
6. Confirm only after reviewing terms.
7. Save confirmation/reference and track the refund.
8. If self-service fails, contact official support before the cutoff.

CHANGE/RESCHEDULE
Open Manage Booking; choose change if available; select the permitted alternative; review change fee and fare difference; check all segments/passengers; confirm and save revised itinerary. Some tickets are non-changeable. A lower replacement fare does not always generate a refund.

AIRLINE-CANCELLED FLIGHT
1. Check official airline messages and booking.
2. Review the airline's offered alternative or refund options.
3. Keep cancellation/delay notices and relevant receipts.
4. Contact official support if options are unclear.
5. Check applicable passenger-rights rules for eligibility.
Compensation, meals, accommodation and alternatives depend on regulation, cause, notice, route and conditions. Do not guarantee compensation without checking the applicable rule.

REFUND TIMING
Refund eligibility, airline initiation and money posting to bank/card are different stages. Timing depends on airline, booking channel and payment provider. Do not promise a fixed number of days from static knowledge.

SPECIAL CASES
- Non-refundable fare: treatment of taxes/charges depends on terms and applicable law.
- Medical emergency: documentation/review may be required; no automatic refund promise.
- Name correction differs from ticket transfer to another person.
- Third-party booking: contact the ticket issuer unless the airline instructs otherwise.
- Failed payment: verify whether a ticket was issued before paying again.
- Travel insurance claims are separate and follow insurer policy.
