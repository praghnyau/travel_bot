AIRWAYS — DIRECT ANSWERS TO COMMON QUESTIONS

TRAVEL BOOKING STEPS
1. Search the airline's official website/app or a trusted booking platform using your origin, destination, travel date and passenger count.
2. Compare times, stops, airports, fare type, baggage allowance, seat options and change/cancellation conditions.
3. Enter passenger details to match the required travel ID; use passport details for international trips.
4. Review the total fare, taxes, fees, baggage, and add-ons, then pay through the official channel.
5. Wait for the confirmed itinerary and booking reference; save them and check that every flight segment is correct.
6. Check in separately and follow the airline's current baggage, terminal, and airport cutoff instructions.
SOURCE: https://www.civilaviation.gov.in/ministry-documents/passenger-charter-of-rights

CANCELLATION PROCESS
1. Open the airline or booking platform where the ticket was purchased and choose Manage Booking/My Trips.
2. Retrieve the booking through that channel; if booked through an agent, contact the ticket issuer.
3. Review the cancellation deadline, fee, refundable charges, refund method, and affected passengers/segments.
4. Confirm only after checking those terms; save the cancellation reference and track the refund with the provider.
5. If the airline cancelled the flight, follow its official notice for the available rebooking or refund options.
Do not assume a fixed fee, refund amount, compensation, or refund timeline; these depend on the fare and current rules.
SOURCES: https://www.civilaviation.gov.in/ministry-documents/passenger-charter-of-rights ; https://www.dgca.gov.in/

TRAVEL DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
1. For a domestic India flight, bring your ticket/boarding information and an ID accepted by the airline and airport.
2. For an international trip, check destination and transit rules for your nationality and itinerary.
3. Confirm passport validity and get any required visa, e-visa, travel authorization, or transit visa.
4. Check whether onward travel, accommodation, funds, health records, or other supporting documents are required.
5. Make sure the ticket name matches your travel document and keep required documents accessible.
Requirements vary by route and nationality; a ticket does not guarantee entry.
SOURCES: https://www.mea.gov.in/ ; https://www.iatatravelcentre.com/

COMMON AIR TRAVEL POLICIES
1. Check your fare rules before paying: baggage, seat selection, changes, cancellations, and refunds vary by ticket.
2. Follow the airline's check-in, bag-drop, and boarding cutoffs; they vary by airport and route.
3. Check baggage allowances and restricted-item rules for each flight segment.
4. Confirm passenger-name rules and request any special assistance or approval before travel.
5. For disruptions or baggage issues, keep notices and receipts and contact the airline through official channels.
SOURCE: https://www.civilaviation.gov.in/ministry-documents/passenger-charter-of-rights
