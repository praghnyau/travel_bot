INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL — DOCUMENT AND TRANSIT CHECKLIST

This checklist is explanatory, not a visa decision. Requirements depend on passport nationality, destination, transit country, residence status, purpose and travel date.

BEFORE BOOKING
1. Confirm passport nationality and destination.
2. Check passport validity and blank-page requirements with the destination government's official source.
3. Confirm visa/e-visa/ETA requirements for the destination and every transit country.
4. Check if transit requires immigration, terminal/airport change, baggage collection/recheck or overnight stay.
5. Verify onward/return ticket, accommodation, funds, invitation, study/work, health or other documents if required.
6. Confirm the airline can carry the traveller on the itinerary.
7. Match ticket name to passport.

KEEP ACCESSIBLE
Passport and visa/entry authorization; itinerary/boarding pass; transit documents; accommodation/onward travel details if required; documents for minors/special cases; insurance/emergency contacts if applicable. Do not post passport numbers or visa barcodes publicly.

TRANSIT VISA
Do not assume airside transit automatically means no visa. Requirements may depend on nationality, airport, terminal, layover duration, baggage and ticket structure. Verify with the official government source and airline.

BORDER CONTROL
Airline staff may refuse boarding if documents are missing/invalid. Border authorities make final entry decisions. The chatbot must not guarantee entry.
