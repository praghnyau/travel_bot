INDIAN RAILWAYS — REVIEWED STEP-BY-STEP ANSWERS

TRAVEL BOOKING STEPS
Step 1: Open the official IRCTC e-ticketing website
Open a web browser and go to https://www.irctc.co.in/.
Use IRCTC's official e-ticketing service or an authorized booking channel.

Step 2: Sign in or create an account
Enter your IRCTC username and password to sign in.
If you do not have an account, use the registration option and complete the requested verification.

Step 3: Enter the journey details
On the travel-planning page, enter the From and To stations and select the correct stations from the available choices.
Choose the travel date and any applicable quota, then submit the search.

Step 4: Compare trains and availability
Review the trains shown for the route, then open a train to check its route and timings.
Select an available class to view its fare and seat status. Choose Book Now only after checking the details shown.

Step 5: Enter passenger details
Check the train and station names displayed on the reservation page.
Enter each passenger's name, age, sex, and berth preference if requested. Review every entry before continuing.

Step 6: Review and pay
Check the passenger details, journey information, availability, and total fare including displayed service charges.
Select a payment option and follow the bank/payment provider instructions. Wait for the result; don't repeat payment while it is processing.

Step 7: Confirm and save the ticket
After successful booking, note the PNR and open the Electronic Reservation Slip (ERS).
Save or print the ERS. If confirmation is unclear after payment, check Booked Ticket History before attempting another booking.

Step 8: Check before travelling
Check each passenger's current PNR status and carry the ticket/ERS and accepted original photo ID required by current Railway rules.
An e-ticket that remains fully waitlisted after chart preparation is not valid for travel under IRCTC guidance.
SOURCES: https://contents.irctc.co.in/en/bookEticket.html ; https://contents.irctc.co.in/en/etktfaq.html

CANCELLATION PROCESS
Step 1: Open Booked Ticket History
Sign in to the account used for the booking and open My Transactions/Booked Ticket History.

Step 2: Select the ticket and passengers
Choose the correct PNR/ticket. If partial cancellation is supported, select only the passengers who will not travel.

Step 3: Review the refund shown
Check the ticket status, class, quota, and cancellation timing. These details affect the applicable refund and any TDR requirements.

Step 4: Confirm and keep the updated ticket
Submit the cancellation and save the receipt. For a partial cancellation, save a fresh ERS for the passengers still travelling.

Step 5: Track the refund
Check the original payment route and follow current IRCTC instructions if an online TDR is required.
SOURCES: https://contents.irctc.co.in/en/eticketCancel.html ; https://contents.irctc.co.in/en/etktfaq.html

TRAVEL DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
Step 1: Keep the valid ticket available
Carry the ticket/ERS/SMS accepted for the booking and keep its details accessible during the journey.

Step 2: Carry accepted original photo ID
Check IRCTC's current accepted-ID list. Do not assume that every student ID, photocopy, or phone image is accepted.

Step 3: Verify passenger status
Check PNR status for every passenger before departure; a PNR alone does not confirm a berth.

Step 4: Follow journey instructions
Keep ticket and ID ready for inspection and follow instructions from railway staff.
SOURCES: https://contents.irctc.co.in/en/etktfaq.html ; https://indianrailways.gov.in/

COMMON RAIL TRAVEL POLICIES
Step 1: Check each passenger's ticket status
Follow the current Railway rules for confirmed, RAC, or waitlisted status. Fully waitlisted e-tickets remaining waitlisted after chart preparation are not valid for travel under IRCTC guidance.

Step 2: Check the applicable fare and quota conditions
Cancellation deductions and special-quota rules can depend on ticket status, class, quota, and timing. Check the current official rules for the booking.

Step 3: Verify permitted changes
Name transfers, boarding-point changes, concessions, and quota rules have restrictions. Confirm eligibility with IRCTC before relying on a change.

Step 4: Check baggage conditions
Verify the current class-based baggage allowance before carrying heavy or oversized items.
SOURCES: https://contents.irctc.co.in/en/etktfaq.html ; https://indianrailways.gov.in/
