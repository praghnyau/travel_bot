BOOKING STEPS
1. Sign in to official IRCTC e-ticketing or use an authorized booking channel.
2. Search source/destination stations, travel date, class and quota.
3. Compare train, departure/arrival time, duration, class, quota, fare and availability.
4. Enter passenger details accurately.
5. Review booking summary and contact details.
6. Pay using a supported method.
7. Save PNR, ticket/ERS, transaction reference and confirmation.
8. Check final status before travelling.

PNR AND STATUS
PNR means Passenger Name Record and is a booking reference; it does not itself guarantee a confirmed berth.
CNF = confirmed. RAC = Reservation Against Cancellation, with travel arrangements governed by Railway rules. WL = waitlisted. Different passengers on the same PNR can have different statuses. Chart preparation finalizes reservation charts; timing can vary.
IRCTC guidance says a fully waitlisted e-ticket that remains waitlisted after chart preparation is not valid for travel.

SPECIAL QUOTAS
Tatkal, Premium Tatkal, concessions and other quotas can have distinct eligibility, booking timing and cancellation rules. Do not apply ordinary-quota rules automatically.

TRAVEL-DAY CHECKLIST
Verify train/date, boarding station, coach/class, PNR and passenger-by-passenger status. Check official service/platform updates. Carry accepted original ID and valid ticket/ERS/SMS as required.

LIVE STATUS
A static knowledge base cannot check current PNR or live train status. Direct the user to official IRCTC/Railway services. Avoid asking the user to post a full PNR publicly.
