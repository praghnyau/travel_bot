E-TICKET CANCELLATION
1. Sign in to the account used to book.
2. Open Booked Ticket History/My Transactions (labels may change).
3. Select the ticket/PNR.
4. Select passenger(s) to cancel if partial cancellation is supported.
5. Review the cancellation/refund details shown.
6. Confirm cancellation.
7. Save cancellation receipt and updated ticket for remaining passengers.
8. Track the refund through the original payment channel.

CHARGES
Deductions depend on status, class, time before departure, quota and current rules. Do not quote old numeric tables as current; check the official cancellation page and booking-specific terms.

AFTER CHART PREPARATION / TDR
Normal e-ticket cancellation is generally available online until chart preparation. Certain cases may require an online TDR (Ticket Deposit Receipt/claim) and Railway review. The reason and filing deadline depend on current rules. Filing a TDR does not guarantee a refund.
General steps:
1. Sign in to the official account and locate the booking.
2. Choose the TDR/refund-claim option if available.
3. Select the accurate reason.
4. Submit within the applicable deadline.
5. Save the claim reference and track the decision.

COMMON CASES
- Confirmed tickets: deduction depends on cancellation timing, class and current rules.
- RAC/waitlisted tickets: separate rules/cutoffs can apply.
- Fully waitlisted e-ticket after chart preparation: IRCTC guidance says it is not valid for travel; refund follows applicable rules.
- Confirmed Tatkal e-ticket: published rules generally restrict refunds on cancellation, subject to current rules/exceptions. Verify before answering.
- Train cancelled, delayed, diverted or passenger did not travel: use the specific current rule and correct claim reason.
- Counter paper tickets: procedures differ from online e-tickets.

REFUND TIMING
Eligibility, claim approval, refund initiation and money reaching the account are different stages. Timing depends on provider/payment method. Never guarantee a fixed credit date.
