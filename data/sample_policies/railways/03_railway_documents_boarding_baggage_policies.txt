INDIAN RAILWAYS — DOCUMENTS, BOARDING, BAGGAGE AND POLICIES

IDENTITY DOCUMENTS
IRCTC rules require passengers to follow the current accepted identity-document list. Published lists have included passport, voter photo ID, driving licence, PAN, Aadhaar, specified government photo IDs and other listed documents subject to current rules. Carry the accepted original where required. Do not assume every bank/student ID, photocopy or image on a phone is accepted.
Official FAQ: https://contents.irctc.co.in/en/etktfaq.html

BOARDING
1. Check PNR and status for every passenger.
2. Confirm train number/name, date, boarding station and class.
3. Check platform/service status using official displays/enquiry.
4. Reach the station with sufficient time.
5. Keep valid ticket/ERS/SMS and accepted ID accessible.
6. Find the correct coach/berth and follow staff instructions.

TICKET VALIDITY AND CHANGES
A valid ticket and permitted status are required. IRCTC guidance says a fully waitlisted e-ticket remaining waitlisted after chart preparation is not valid for travel. RAC and partly confirmed bookings need status checking. Tickets are not freely transferable; name and boarding-point changes have restricted procedures and deadlines.

BAGGAGE
Rules depend on class, weight, size and whether luggage is carried or booked separately. Heavy/oversized items may require formal luggage booking. Do not state a universal free allowance without knowing class and current rules. Keep valuables secure and do not carry prohibited dangerous/inflammable/explosive items contrary to Railway rules.

COMMON POLICIES
Concessions require eligibility/proof. Name transfer, boarding-point changes, break journey and quota rules are restricted. Catering varies. Contact Railway staff for safety incidents, lost property and service issues.

FAQ
Q: Is PNR enough?
A: PNR is a reference; keep valid ticket/ERS/SMS and accepted original ID.
Q: Can I travel on a fully waitlisted e-ticket after charting?
A: IRCTC guidance says it is not valid for travel.
Q: Can I change passenger name?
A: Only limited cases may be allowed; check current official rules.
Q: Can I cancel after chart preparation?
A: A TDR may be required in eligible cases; follow current deadline.
