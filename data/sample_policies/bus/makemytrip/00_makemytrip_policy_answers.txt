TRAVEL BOOKING STEPS
Step 1: Open the official MakeMyTrip booking channel
Open a web browser or the MakeMyTrip app and go to https://www.makemytrip.com/bus-tickets/.
You can also book through the MakeMyTrip website or app. Use the provider's official channel so the ticket and support route match the selected provider.

Step 2: Enter the journey details
Enter your departure city and destination city.
Choose the travel date and passenger count, then search for available buses.

Step 3: Compare available options
Compare bus operator/service, departure and arrival times, journey duration, bus type, available seats, and exact boarding and drop points.
Open service details to verify the pickup location and operator's conditions.

Step 4: Select a service and review its rules
Choose the service and seat if available.
Before continuing, review the selected bus operator's conditions and any optional Free Cancellation or TripAssured benefit shown for the ticket. Check whether changes or cancellation are allowed and confirm the pickup/drop points.

Step 5: Enter passenger details
Enter passenger names and contact details as requested.
Make sure ticket details match the ID required by the bus operator.
Review names, dates, and contact details carefully before continuing.

Step 6: Review the total and pay
Check the operator/service, passenger details, seat, boarding/drop points, fare, fees, baggage terms, and selected add-ons.
Pay using an option displayed by MakeMyTrip. Wait for the booking result; do not refresh or submit payment again while it is processing.

Step 7: Confirm and save the booking
Confirm that payment resulted in a booking and save the bus e-ticket and booking reference in MakeMyTrip My Trips.
If confirmation is unclear, check the provider's booking history before trying to pay again.

Step 8: Check travel-day instructions
Before departure, check the pickup point, reporting time, accepted ticket format, and operator's instructions shown in My Trips.
SOURCES: https://www.makemytrip.com/bus-tickets/ ; https://www.makemytrip.com/legal/in/eng/user_agreement.html

CANCELLATION PROCESS
1. Open My Trips on the MakeMyTrip website/app and select the bus booking.
2. Review the cancellation charges and refund amount shown for the ticket; operator terms and any selected add-on can affect the result.
3. Select full or partial cancellation if the booking allows it, then confirm after reviewing the amount.
4. Save the confirmation and check refund status in My Trips.
Optional Free Cancellation or TripAssured applies only to eligible bookings where the benefit was selected; review its ticket-specific terms.
SOURCES: https://www.makemytrip.com/promos/gt-bus-free-cancellation-251123.html ; https://www.makemytrip.com/support/in/eng/contact-us.php

TRAVEL DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
1. Keep the MakeMyTrip bus ticket available and check its accepted ticket format.
2. Carry valid government-issued photo ID that matches the ticket passenger details.
3. Confirm the exact boarding point and reach it by the reporting time stated for the booking.
4. For service, route, or pickup questions, follow the booking instructions and contact the bus operator or MakeMyTrip support as appropriate.
SOURCE: https://www.makemytrip.com/legal/in/eng/user_agreement.html

COMMON MAKEMYTRIP POLICIES
1. MakeMyTrip acts as a booking platform; bus operation details and service conditions are supplied by the bus operator.
2. The ticket's cancellation terms determine applicable charges; check them in My Trips before confirming.
3. Optional Free Cancellation/TripAssured benefits apply only to qualifying bookings and under their own displayed terms.
4. Bus tickets and passenger changes can be restricted; check the selected ticket for partial cancellation, transfer, and amendment options.
