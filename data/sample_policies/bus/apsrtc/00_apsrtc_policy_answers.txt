APSRTC OFFICIAL PORTAL — REVIEWED ANSWERS

TRAVEL BOOKING STEPS
Step 1: Open the official APSRTC booking channel
Open a web browser or the APSRTC app and go to https://www.apsrtconline.in/.
You can also book through the APSRTC official portal/app or an authorized channel. Use the provider's official channel so the ticket and support route match the selected provider.

Step 2: Enter the journey details
Enter your departure city and destination city.
Choose the travel date and passenger count, then search for available buses.

Step 3: Compare available options
Compare bus operator/service, departure and arrival times, journey duration, bus type, available seats, and exact boarding and drop points.
Open service details to verify the pickup location and operator's conditions.

Step 4: Select a service and review its rules
Choose the service and seat if available.
Before continuing, review the selected APSRTC service, seat, fare, boarding point, and current cancellation terms. Check whether changes or cancellation are allowed and confirm the pickup/drop points.

Step 5: Enter passenger details
Enter passenger names and contact details as requested.
Make sure ticket details match the ID required by the bus operator.
Review names, dates, and contact details carefully before continuing.

Step 6: Review the total and pay
Check the operator/service, passenger details, seat, boarding/drop points, fare, fees, baggage terms, and selected add-ons.
Pay using an option displayed by APSRTC. Wait for the booking result; do not refresh or submit payment again while it is processing.

Step 7: Confirm and save the booking
Confirm that payment resulted in a booking and save the APSRTC ticket and booking reference.
If confirmation is unclear, check the provider's booking history before trying to pay again.

Step 8: Check travel-day instructions
Before departure, check the ticket's pickup point, reporting time, accepted format, and service instructions.
SOURCES: https://www.apsrtconline.in/ ; https://www.apsrtconline.in/oprs-web/home/terms.do

CANCELLATION PROCESS
1. Open the official APSRTC portal/app and retrieve the booking using its ticket details.
2. Review the current cancellation slab and refundable amount shown for the service before confirming.
3. Submit the cancellation and save its reference/receipt.
4. Track the refund using the official portal or support channel.
APSRTC terms can distinguish fare from other charges and may set different rules for linked or multi-segment tickets. Check the current terms page; do not rely on a memorized percentage.
SOURCE: https://www.apsrtconline.in/oprs-web/home/terms.do

TRAVEL DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
1. Carry the APSRTC ticket/booking reference in the format accepted for that service.
2. Carry valid photo ID if required and make sure passenger details match the ticket.
3. Check the ticket for pickup point, reporting time, and any service-specific boarding instructions.
4. For uncertain ID or ticket requirements, confirm with APSRTC using its official portal/support.
SOURCES: https://www.apsrtconline.in/ ; https://www.apsrtconline.in/oprs-web/home/terms.do

COMMON APSRTC POLICIES
1. APSRTC's current terms control cancellations, deductions, and refund eligibility; review them for the exact service and ticket.
2. Linked/multi-segment tickets can have special cancellation restrictions; do not assume each segment can be cancelled independently.
3. Use the official portal to manage the ticket and retain its cancellation or payment reference.
4. Follow the ticket for boarding, reporting, ID, and service instructions.
SOURCE: https://www.apsrtconline.in/oprs-web/home/terms.do
