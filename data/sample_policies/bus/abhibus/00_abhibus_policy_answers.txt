ABHIBUS — REVIEWED PLATFORM ANSWERS

TRAVEL BOOKING STEPS
Step 1: Open the official AbhiBus booking channel
Open a web browser or the AbhiBus app and go to https://www.abhibus.com/.
You can also book through the AbhiBus website or app. Use the provider's official channel so the ticket and support route match the selected provider.

Step 2: Enter the journey details
Enter your departure city and destination city.
Choose the travel date and passenger count, then search for available buses.

Step 3: Compare available options
Compare bus operator/service, departure and arrival times, journey duration, bus type, available seats, and exact boarding and drop points.
Open service details to verify the pickup location and operator's conditions.

Step 4: Select a service and review its rules
Choose the service and seat if available.
Before continuing, review the selected bus partner's cancellation, baggage, boarding, and rescheduling terms. Review optional Free Cancellation or Abhi Assured benefits separately. Check whether changes or cancellation are allowed and confirm the pickup/drop points.

Step 5: Enter passenger details
Enter passenger names and contact details as requested.
Make sure ticket details match the ID required by the bus operator.
Review names, dates, and contact details carefully before continuing.

Step 6: Review the total and pay
Check the operator/service, passenger details, seat, boarding/drop points, fare, fees, baggage terms, and selected add-ons.
Pay using an option displayed by AbhiBus. Wait for the booking result; do not refresh or submit payment again while it is processing.

Step 7: Confirm and save the booking
Confirm that payment resulted in a booking and save the AbhiBus e-ticket and booking ID.
If confirmation is unclear, check the provider's booking history before trying to pay again.

Step 8: Check travel-day instructions
Before departure, check the pickup point, reporting time, accepted ticket format, and operator's instructions shown on the ticket.
SOURCES: https://www.abhibus.com/bus-ticket-booking/ ; https://www.abhibus.com/faq

CANCELLATION PROCESS
1. Open AbhiBus cancellation or My Bookings and retrieve the ticket using the booking details requested.
2. Check the bus partner's cutoff and cancellation amount shown for this ticket; the operator's rule determines the base refund.
3. Choose an available refund route, review any AbhiCash/UPI/original-payment option shown, and confirm only if the terms are clear.
4. Save the cancellation confirmation and track the refund using AbhiBus and the payment provider.
Optional Free Cancellation/Abhi Assured benefits apply only if selected and if the booking meets the displayed conditions.
SOURCES: https://www.abhibus.com/cancellation ; https://www.abhibus.com/faq ; https://www.abhibus.com/abhi-assured

TRAVEL DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
1. Keep the AbhiBus e-ticket available as a digital copy or printout, as allowed by the operator.
2. Carry valid government-issued photo ID for each passenger and match the passenger name to the booking.
3. Follow the ticket for boarding point, reporting time, and any operator-specific instructions.
4. Contact AbhiBus support for booking issues; contact the bus partner for service or boarding details when directed by the ticket.
SOURCE: https://www.abhibus.com/faq

COMMON ABHIBUS POLICIES
1. AbhiBus is the booking platform; the bus partner's cancellation cutoff, refund, baggage, and boarding rules can vary by service.
2. The ticket/booking confirmation shows the applicable cancellation terms; check it before cancelling.
3. AbhiBus Free Cancellation and Abhi Assured are optional benefits. Their eligibility, cutoff, refund scope, and claim steps depend on the selected benefit's terms.
4. AbhiBus says confirmed ticket changes such as date, time, route, or passenger name may not be supported; check the booking's current options before cancelling and rebooking.
