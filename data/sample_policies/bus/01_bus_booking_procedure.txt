BUS TRAVEL — BOOKING PROCEDURE

SCOPE
Private buses, online platforms, and state road transport corporations in India. Exact terms vary by operator.

ONLINE BOOKING
1. Open the official operator app/site or a recognized booking platform.
2. Enter origin, destination, travel date and passenger count.
3. Compare service/operator, departure and arrival time, duration, bus type, amenities, seat layout, pickup/drop locations and fare.
4. Read the service-specific cancellation, rescheduling, baggage and passenger rules.
5. Select seats if the service permits seat selection.
6. Enter passenger and contact details exactly as requested.
7. Confirm pickup and drop points; check address and landmark carefully.
8. Review base fare, taxes, platform/service fees, insurance/add-ons and total.
9. Pay through a supported method. Never share OTP/PIN with a caller.
10. Wait for confirmed ticket and save ticket number/booking reference, SMS/email and receipt.
11. If payment succeeds but no ticket appears, check booking history and official support before paying again.

COUNTER BOOKING
Use an authorized operator counter or agent. Confirm route, date, service, seat, pickup point, fare and cancellation terms before paying. Keep ticket and receipt.

BEFORE DEPARTURE
Check service status and pickup instructions on travel day. Keep ticket and required ID accessible. Reach the pickup point early enough for the operator's cutoff. Confirm the correct bus with staff.

TERMS
Pickup/boarding point: where passenger joins. Drop point: destination stop. Operator: company running the bus. Platform: site/app selling the ticket. No-show: passenger fails to board by required time. Partial cancellation: cancelling only some passengers, if allowed. Rescheduling: changing date/service if offered.

COMMON RULES
- Pickup points can differ from a bus terminal; read the ticket carefully.
- Ticket transfer and passenger-name changes are restricted unless the operator permits them.
- Amenities, seats and boarding instructions are subject to service terms.
- Do not assume every bus ticket is refundable or reschedulable.
