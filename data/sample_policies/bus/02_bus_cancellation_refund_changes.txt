BUS TRAVEL — CANCELLATION, REFUNDS AND CHANGES

GENERAL RULE
Fees and cutoffs vary by operator, route, booking platform, ticket type and time remaining before departure. Use the policy shown for the actual ticket.

ONLINE CANCELLATION
1. Open the app/site used for booking.
2. Go to My Bookings/My Trips/Cancellation.
3. Retrieve the ticket using the requested booking reference/contact details on the official channel.
4. Select the ticket and passengers to cancel if partial cancellation is supported.
5. Review the cancellation deadline, deduction, refund estimate, non-refundable fees and refund destination.
6. Confirm only after reviewing the terms.
7. Save the cancellation confirmation/reference.
8. Track the refund through the provider/payment method.
If online cancellation fails, contact official support or the authorized counter before the cutoff. A message to a driver or unofficial agent may not cancel the ticket.

REFUND CONCEPT
The amount returned may be the eligible fare minus the applicable cancellation penalty and non-refundable charges. This is explanatory only, not a universal formula; actual terms control.

MISSED BUS
A late arrival may be treated as a no-show and can make the ticket non-refundable. Check ticket terms and contact official support promptly. Never promise a refund.

OPERATOR CANCELS OR DOES NOT ARRIVE
1. Save ticket, messages and notices.
2. Contact the operator/platform using official channels.
3. Ask about the applicable alternative-service or refund process.
4. Record complaint/reference number.
5. Confirm who is responsible for processing the refund.

RESCHEDULING
1. Open the booking and choose reschedule/change if offered.
2. Select an eligible new service/date.
3. Review change fee and fare difference.
4. Confirm and save the revised ticket.
Some tickets cannot be changed; some allow changes only under conditions.

PARTIAL CANCELLATION
If supported, select only passengers not travelling. Verify the updated ticket and seats for remaining passengers.

PROVIDER NOTES
