BUS TRAVEL — DOCUMENTS, BOARDING, BAGGAGE AND POLICIES

DOCUMENTS
- Keep the e-ticket, SMS, app ticket or booking confirmation accepted by the operator.
- Carry valid photo ID where required; accepted IDs vary.
- Ensure the passenger identity matches booking details as required.
- Carry concession/special-category proof if applicable.
- Cross-border bus trips may require passport, visa and customs compliance.

BOARDING PROCEDURE
1. Check the service status and exact pickup location on travel day.
2. Arrive early; follow the ticket/operator's stated reporting time.
3. Keep ticket and required ID ready.
4. Confirm bus/operator and destination with staff.
5. Use the assigned seat if one is allocated.
6. If late or the pickup point is unclear, contact official support immediately.
Do not assume the bus will wait for a late passenger.

BAGGAGE
Operator rules can limit bag count, size, weight and item type; extra charges may apply. Keep valuables and documents with you. Confirm in advance for oversized luggage, bicycles, sports equipment, pets and fragile items. Do not carry prohibited or dangerous items contrary to law/operator rules.

COMMON POLICIES
- Cancellation deductions and deadlines vary.
- Rescheduling is available only on some tickets.
- Missed-bus refunds are not guaranteed.
- Seat selection and amenities depend on service terms.
- Rules for pets, smoking, alcohol, food and passenger conduct vary.
- Operator may refuse unsafe luggage or unsafe conduct under applicable rules.

FAQ
Q: Is a printout necessary?
A: Many services accept mobile tickets, but follow the actual ticket instructions.
Q: Can I change pickup point?
A: Only if the operator permits it; contact official support before departure.
Q: Can I get a refund after missing the bus?
A: It depends on ticket terms and circumstances.
Q: How much luggage is allowed?
A: Ask the operator; there is no universal allowance for all buses.
