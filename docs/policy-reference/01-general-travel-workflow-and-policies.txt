GENERAL TRAVEL WORKFLOW AND POLICIES

A. BEFORE BOOKING
1. Confirm origin, destination, travel date, departure time preference, passenger count, and one-way/return.
2. Compare departure/arrival times, duration, stops, terminal/station/pickup point, service type, seat/berth, amenities, baggage, accessibility and total cost.
3. Enter passenger names and details accurately as required by the operator.
4. Read cancellation, refund, rescheduling, no-show, baggage, special-item, child/minor, and assistance policies before payment.
5. Check whether the site is the operator or a third-party booking platform.
6. Review base fare, taxes, platform/convenience fees, optional add-ons and total.
7. Pay only through the official/recognized channel.
8. Wait for a confirmed ticket and save booking reference, ticket/itinerary, receipt, contact details and policy shown at booking.

B. COMMON BOOKING PROBLEMS
Payment succeeded but no ticket: check booking history, SMS/email and payment status; do not immediately pay again. Contact official support with transaction reference.
Wrong name/date/route: contact the ticket issuer promptly. Name correction is not the same as transferring a ticket to another person.
Duplicate booking: check confirmation records and ask official support about duplicate-payment/booking policy.
Failed payment: verify whether a ticket was actually issued before trying again.

C. CANCELLATION AND REFUND
1. Open the booking channel used.
2. Find My Bookings/My Trips/Booked Tickets.
3. Select the booking and passenger/segment if partial cancellation is allowed.
4. Review the actual cancellation deadline, deduction, non-refundable fees/add-ons, refund method and estimated processing information.
5. Confirm only after reviewing the displayed terms.
6. Save cancellation confirmation/reference.
7. Track the refund using the provider/payment channel.
Eligibility, refund initiation, and the date funds reach the bank are different stages.

D. CHANGES/RESCHEDULING
1. Open the booking and select change/reschedule if available.
2. Choose the permitted alternative.
3. Review change fee, fare difference, restrictions and effects on other segments/passengers.
4. Confirm only after checking the total.
5. Save the revised ticket.
Some tickets cannot be changed. A lower replacement fare does not automatically mean a refund.

E. BOARDING
1. Check service status on the day of travel.
2. Verify exact pickup point, station, terminal, platform and gate.
3. Arrive before the provider's stated cutoff.
4. Keep ticket/boarding pass and accepted ID ready.
5. Follow security, baggage and staff instructions.
6. Confirm the final platform/gate/vehicle using official displays or messages.

F. DOCUMENTS — GENERAL
- Bus: ticket/booking confirmation; accepted photo ID where required by operator.
- Indian Railways: valid ticket/ERS/SMS and accepted identity document according to current Railway rules.
- Domestic flight: accepted photo ID plus ticket/boarding information under airline/airport rules.
- International flight: passport, required visa/e-visa/ETA, transit authorization and any destination-specific documents.
- Concessions/special categories: eligibility proof where required.
Never assume one document is accepted by every operator.

G. BAGGAGE AND SPECIAL ITEMS
Limits depend on transport type, operator, class/fare, route, size, weight and item. Airline cabin and checked baggage are separate. Oversized luggage, pets, sports gear, instruments, medicines, batteries and dangerous goods can have special rules. Check the official current list; never advise hiding restricted items.

H. DELAY, CANCELLATION BY OPERATOR, OR MISSED DEPARTURE
Keep notices, ticket, receipts and support reference. Contact official support promptly. Passenger-requested cancellation differs from operator cancellation. Refund, alternate travel, accommodation or compensation depends on transport type, reason, notice, ticket and applicable law. Missed-departure/no-show rules may remove refund eligibility. Separate connecting bookings may not be protected when the first service is delayed.

I. INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL
Requirements depend on nationality, destination, transit country, travel purpose and date. Check official government/embassy sources for passport validity, visa/ETA, transit visa, onward ticket, funds, accommodation, health requirements and entry conditions. A ticket does not guarantee admission; border authorities decide entry.

J. PRIVACY
Never request passwords, OTPs, card PIN/CVV, full card numbers, or full passport/ID numbers. Ask only for details needed to explain the rule.
