TRAVEL GLOSSARY

Aggregator/platform: Site/app selling or organizing bookings for operators.
Boarding point: Place where a bus passenger joins, or the station/airport where a journey starts.
Boarding pass: Airline document/credential used to board a flight.
Booking reference: Code used to retrieve a reservation.
Cabin baggage: Luggage carried into the aircraft cabin within limits.
Cancellation charge: Amount deducted when a ticket is cancelled.
Chart preparation: Railway process for finalizing reservation charts.
Checked baggage: Airline luggage handed to the carrier for the aircraft hold.
CNF: Confirmed railway reservation status.
Convenience/platform fee: Booking-platform charge; refundability varies.
E-ticket: Electronic ticket/booking record.
ERS: Electronic Reservation Slip for an IRCTC e-ticket.
Fare difference: Price difference when changing service/date.
Flight segment: One leg of a flight itinerary.
Gate closure: Time after which boarding may not be accepted.
M-ticket: Mobile ticket, where accepted.
No-show: Passenger does not board by required time.
Operating carrier: Airline actually operating a flight.
PNR: Passenger Name Record; booking reference.
RAC: Reservation Against Cancellation; railway status defined by Railway rules.
Refund: Money returned if eligible.
Reschedule: Change ticket to another date/service.
TDR: Ticket Deposit Receipt/claim used in specified Railway refund cases.
Transit visa: Visa that may be required while passing through a country.
WL: Waitlisted railway status.
