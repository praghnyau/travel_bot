MAINTENANCE
- Re-check dynamic cancellation, baggage, ID, check-in and disruption policies monthly or after a reported discrepancy.
- Record URL, date checked, summary of change and reviewer.
- Keep general procedures separate from provider-specific numeric facts.
- If an official page is unavailable, mark the rule “not verified”; do not guess.
- For a particular booking, prioritize the ticket terms and current official provider page.
- This starter set covers bus, Indian Railways and airline travel, including selected India-focused providers. It does not cover every operator worldwide, ferry, metro, taxi, cruise or travel insurance. Add separate sources/files before claiming those are covered.
